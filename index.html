Featured case study · Causal ML

Estimating causal price elasticity with Double Machine Learning

Retail pricing teams need to know how demand actually responds to price, not just how the two move together. I built an end-to-end pipeline that compared a log-log regression baseline with a DML model to isolate the causal effect of price on demand, and the results fed directly into pricing decisions.

  1. IngestRaw retail transactions
  2. CleanStructured cleaning of messy transactional data
  3. BaselineLog-log regression elasticity
  4. DMLResidualize price & demand on controls, then estimate effect
  5. TrackExperiments versioned in MLflow
  6. DeployProduction-style pipeline on Databricks

log(Q) = α + ε·log(P) + g(X) + u   →   ε = % change in demand per 1% change in price

Healthcare ops · Event streams · Decision support

CoverGap

Special Shout-Out · Rutgers Health Hack

Hospitals can have consults waiting while no credentialed, available physician can take them. CoverGap watches a live coverage event stream, detects those gaps before they happen, recommends ranked fixes, and routes each one through clinical-lead approval before acting. Built for the Sevaro Challenge.

  • Detect → Decide → Approve → Act → Update loop over a replayed, de-duplicated, time-ordered event stream
  • Flags demand above each hospital's 90th-percentile baseline and roster gaps projected 120 minutes ahead
  • Ranked Plan A / Plan B interventions with a routing simulator; 38 automated tests
